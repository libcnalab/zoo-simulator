## Sources whose authorship we could not confirm

The rhinoceros and elephant sources above carry a CC BY 4.0 label and a named uploader on
Sketchfab, and that is all we know: neither page says who modelled the animal, and we found no
second source that confirms it. They are used here as published, with the credit the licence
asks for. Before the game is distributed outside this machine the owner should decide whether
that is enough, or whether these two species go back to models made in this repository
(`git log -- assets/animals/rhino.glb assets/animals/elephant.glb` has them).
